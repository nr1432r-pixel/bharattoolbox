Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SpinCash Pro ("SpinCash Pro", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the SpinCash Pro mobile application and related services.

By using SpinCash Pro, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, SpinCash Pro may collect or process the following information:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address associated with your account.
  • Profile picture when provided through supported sign-in services.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Coins, rewards, referral and transaction-related information.
  • Withdrawal-related information submitted by the user.
  • Device and application information.
  • Crash and diagnostic information.
  • Usage and analytics information.

2. Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication

SpinCash Pro may use Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to allow users to create and access their accounts.

When you sign in using Google, information made available by Google to the application may include your name, email address, and profile picture, depending on the permissions and account settings applicable to the sign-in process.

Authentication and account information may be processed using Firebase Authentication.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing authentication and account access.
  • Maintaining coins, rewards and referral records.
  • Processing eligible withdrawal requests.
  • Providing application features and services.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud, misuse or unauthorized activity.
  • Improving application performance and functionality.
  • Understanding application usage and engagement.
  • Diagnosing technical problems and crashes.
  • Communicating important service-related information.

4. Firebase Services

SpinCash Pro may use Firebase services provided by Google, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

These services may process information required to authenticate users, store application data, understand application usage, and identify technical problems.

5. Advertising Services

SpinCash Pro may display advertisements using third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob and Unity Ads.

These advertising providers may collect or receive certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location information, and advertising interaction information, subject to their respective privacy policies and applicable settings.

Advertising providers may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, or improve advertisements, where permitted by applicable law and user settings.

6. Rewards, Coins and Withdrawals

SpinCash Pro may maintain information relating to coins, rewards, referrals, earning activities and withdrawal requests.

This information is used to maintain the user's reward balance, verify eligible activities and process applicable requests.

Rewards and withdrawal availability are subject to the application's applicable rules, eligibility requirements, limits and terms.

7. Referral Program

If referral features are available, information relating to referral activity may be processed to calculate and provide eligible referral rewards and to prevent abuse or fraudulent activity.

8. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be stored using cloud infrastructure and services provided by third-party service providers, including Firebase and Google Cloud services.

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information as a product.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary to operate SpinCash Pro, including authentication, cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting, advertising, security, and other infrastructure services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, security, and integrity of users and the service.

10. Third-Party Services

SpinCash Pro may use third-party services whose own privacy policies govern their processing of information.

Examples may include:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

Users should review the privacy policies of these third-party providers for additional information about their practices.

11. Children's Privacy

SpinCash Pro is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the service in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce applicable terms.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the applicable service and law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You may also manage certain advertising and Google account privacy settings through the relevant Google or device settings.
