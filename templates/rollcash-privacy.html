Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RollCash collects, uses, protects, and handles information when you use the RollCash application and related services.

Last Updated: September 23, 2026

About RollCash

RollCash is provided as a free application. The Service is provided by RollCash at no cost and is intended for use as is. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, storage, and disclosure of information when you use our Service.

01 Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may collect certain information that you provide directly or that is generated through your use of the application.

This information may include:

  • Your name and email address associated with your account.
  • Account and authentication information.
  • Device-related information required to operate and secure the application.
  • Information related to rewards, referrals, activity, and account usage.

Information collected may be used to provide and improve the Service, manage user accounts, maintain rewards and referrals, support applicable withdrawal functionality, maintain security, and improve the overall user experience.

02 Google Sign-In

RollCash uses Google Sign-In to provide secure authentication and account access.

When you choose to sign in with Google, we may access basic account information such as your name and email address.

This information is used for account creation, authentication, login purposes, and to securely associate your RollCash account and applicable rewards or activity with your account.

Google Account Information

Google account information is used only for the purposes disclosed in this Privacy Policy and to provide and maintain the RollCash Service.

03 Firebase Services

RollCash uses Google Firebase services to provide authentication, data storage, analytics, and application stability features.

🔐
Firebase Authentication Used to securely manage user accounts and authentication.
☁️
Cloud Firestore Used to store applicable account, reward, referral, and application data.
📊
Firebase Analytics Used to understand application usage and improve features and experience.
🛠️
Firebase Crashlytics Used to identify crashes and improve application stability.

Firebase services may collect device information, usage information, and application interaction data in accordance with their applicable privacy practices.

04 Advertisements

RollCash displays advertisements using third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect information such as device identifiers, Advertising ID, IP address, and usage information for purposes such as serving personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and supporting the Service.

05 Data Security

We value your trust in providing your personal information and strive to use commercially reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Service.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure and reliable. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

06 Third-Party Services

RollCash may use third-party services that may collect information in connection with providing, maintaining, analyzing, securing, or improving the Service.

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Firebase
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Sign-In

07 Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take reasonable steps to delete such information from our systems.

08 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes to this Privacy Policy.
