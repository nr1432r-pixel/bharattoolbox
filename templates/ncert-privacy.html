Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NCERT All In One handles information, educational resources, authentication, third-party services, advertising, and offline access when you use the application.

Last Updated: August 2026

About NCERT All In One

NCERT All In One is an independent educational platform designed to provide students with a convenient interface for accessing educational resources and study tools.

01

Source of Government Information & Disclaimer

NCERT All In One is an independent educational platform. It is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or officially connected with the National Council of Educational Research and Training (NCERT), CBSE, or any government organization in India.

Educational materials, textbooks, and related resources available through the application may be sourced from publicly available educational resources and government portals.

Relevant official educational sources include:

🌐
NCERT Official Portal https://ncert.nic.in/
📚
NCERT Digital Textbooks https://ncert.nic.in/textbook.php
🎓
DIKSHA Portal https://diksha.gov.in/
🏫
ePathshala https://epathshala.nic.in/
Independent Platform

The application provides a convenient interface to access available educational resources. We do not represent any government entity and do not claim official government affiliation.

02

Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, we may collect certain information that you provide when using the application.

This may include:

  • Your name and email address when using Google Sign-In.
  • Information associated with your account.
  • User progress and bookmarks where supported.
  • Device and usage information required for application functionality, security, analytics, and troubleshooting.

Information may be used to manage user progress and bookmarks, provide applicable educational resources, improve application performance and stability, maintain security, and improve the overall user experience.

03

Third-Party Services

NCERT All In One uses trusted third-party services that may process or collect information in accordance with their respective privacy practices.

▶
Google Play Services Used for supported application functionality.
🔥
Firebase Used for authentication, Firestore, Storage, Analytics and other supported app services.
📢
Google AdMob Used to display advertisements that support the free service.

04

Google Sign-In

NCERT All In One may use Google Sign-In for authentication.

When you choose Google Sign-In, we may access basic profile information such as your name and email address for account creation and login purposes.

This information may also be used to securely link applicable bookmarks, progress, and account information to your account.

Use of Google Account Information

Google account information is used for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and to provide, maintain, and secure the relevant application functionality.

05

Firebase Services

We use Firebase services to provide a seamless educational experience and support account, progress, resource, analytics, and stability functionality.

🔐
Firebase Authentication Used to securely manage user accounts.
☁️
Cloud Firestore Used to store applicable user progress, class selections, and favorite books.
📊
Firebase Analytics Used to understand application usage and improve educational features.
🛠️
Firebase Crashlytics Used to monitor crashes and improve application stability.
📚
Firebase Storage Used to provide access to applicable educational PDF resources.
06

Advertisements

NCERT All In One displays advertisements using third-party advertising services, primarily Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may collect information such as device identifiers, Advertising ID, IP address, and usage information to provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements, measure advertising performance, support the Service, and help keep the application available to users.

07

Storage & Offline Access

To provide offline reading, the application may allow educational PDF books or resources to be downloaded and saved on your device.

Downloaded files are intended for offline study and use through the functionality provided by the application.

Device Storage

Any storage permission or access requested by the application is intended to support the download and offline reading functionality.

08

Data Security

We value your trust in providing personal information and strive to use commercially reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Service.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure and reliable. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

09

Children's Privacy

NCERT All In One provides educational content suitable for students.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent where required by applicable law.

If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal information in circumstances where collection was not permitted, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information from our systems.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated "Last Updated" date.

You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes to this Privacy Policy.
